# Plasma Universe Experiment

This is a standalone alternative to the approved Communique 7 baseline 1.0.

It replaces orbital geometry with a Canvas-driven plasma universe: pulsing color fields, animated energy filaments, drifting stars, and particles that flow through a responsive vector field.

The supplied video served only as visual direction and is not included in this package.

To restore the approved version, use `communique-login-v1.0.zip`.
